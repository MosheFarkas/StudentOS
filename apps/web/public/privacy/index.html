Privacy Policy

Effective 6 September 2026

Contexto Agent is an AI study assistant for students. It connects to your Google Classroom, Google Drive and Gmail so that it can answer questions about your own coursework — your assignments, their due dates, the materials your teachers posted, and the files you have written.

This policy explains exactly what data we touch, why we touch it, where it goes and how to get rid of it. It applies to the website at contextoagent.ai and to the Contexto Agent desktop application.

Limited Use. Contexto Agent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google data we access

We ask for each of these separately. Signing in grants only your identity; Classroom, Drive and Gmail are individual connections you turn on yourself, and you can turn any of them off without affecting the others.

Scope What it lets us do, and why we need it
openid, userinfo.email, userinfo.profile Your name, email address and profile picture. Used to create your account and show you which account you are signed in as.
classroom.courses.readonly The list of courses you are enrolled in. The backbone of everything else: without it there is nothing to organise your work around.
classroom.coursework.me.readonly, classroom.student-submissions.me.readonly Your assignments and your own submissions to them, so we can tell you what is due, what you have handed in, and what you still owe.
classroom.announcements.readonly, classroom.topics.readonly, classroom.courseworkmaterials.readonly Announcements, topics and materials your teachers posted, so the agent answers using what your teacher actually said and shared.
classroom.coursework.me Turn in work, take it back, and attach files to a submission, so you can hand an assignment in by asking. We never submit anything without you asking us to in that conversation.
drive.file Read only the individual files you hand over through Google's own file picker. This is the default for Drive, and it grants access to nothing until you pick a specific file.
drive.readonly Read the files in your Drive. Optional, and off unless you switch it on. It lets the agent find your coursework without you locating each file by hand — see Full Drive access below.
gmail.readonly, gmail.modify, gmail.labels Read your mail, and send, reply, label, archive and move messages to trash. This surfaces mail from your school and teachers, and lets the agent reply or tidy up when you ask. We do not request https://mail.google.com/, the only scope that can delete mail permanently, so anything the agent removes stays recoverable in your trash.

Full Drive access

drive.readonly is an optional upgrade, never requested as part of connecting Drive. When you enable it, Contexto Agent scans your Drive to work out which files are schoolwork, and imports only those. Files it judges to be personal, along with files last edited before the current school year, are left alone and their contents are never read. You can revoke it at any time and fall back to handing over files individually.

How we use it

Everything we read goes into your vault — a private, per-student index of your coursework held on our servers. The agent reads your vault to answer your questions. That is the entire purpose.

To produce an answer, the relevant excerpts are sent to a large language model:

  • By default, to OpenAI through its API, using our account.
  • If you supply your own API key, to OpenAI or Anthropic instead, using your account.

These providers process the text solely to generate your response. We do not use your Google data to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models, and we do not permit our providers to do so with it either.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data, and we never have.
  • We do not use it for advertising, or transfer it to advertising platforms.
  • We do not share it with data brokers or information resellers.
  • No human at Contexto Agent reads your Google data, except with your explicit permission to resolve a problem you have reported, or where the law requires it.

Who else sees it

We share your data with a short list of service providers, and only what each needs:

  • OpenAI and, if you choose it, Anthropic — the excerpts needed to answer a question.
  • Our hosting provider — the servers and managed database that store your vault.
  • Telegram — only if you connect it, and only the messages exchanged on that channel.

Beyond that we disclose data only when we are legally required to, or to protect someone's safety.

Data we collect that is not from Google

  • School portals. If you connect a school portal such as Veracross, we store the timetable and assignment data it returns, and your credentials for it encrypted.
  • Your conversations. The messages you exchange with the agent, so your chats survive a refresh.
  • Linked devices. Which computers you have linked to your account.
  • Operational logs. Errors and request timings, used to keep the service running.

Storage and security

  • Data is held in a PostgreSQL database on servers we control.
  • Google OAuth tokens and any API keys you supply are encrypted at rest.
  • All traffic to and from the service is encrypted in transit over HTTPS.
  • Access to production systems is limited to authorised operators and protected by key-based authentication.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can remove it in three ways:

  • Disconnect one integration. In Settings, switch off Classroom, Drive or Gmail. We stop reading it and delete what that integration put in your vault.
  • Revoke our access at Google. Visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Contexto Agent. This cuts off access immediately, from Google's side, and works whether or not you can reach us.
  • Delete your account. Email privacy@contextoagent.ai from the address on the account. We delete your account, your vault, your conversations and your stored tokens within 30 days, and confirm when it is done.

Backups are retained for a further 30 days before being overwritten. Operational logs, which do not contain the content of your Google data, are kept for up to 90 days.

Students and younger users

Contexto Agent is built for students, and some of them are minors. If you are under the age of majority where you live, please read this policy with a parent, guardian or teacher before connecting your accounts. If your Google account is issued by your school, your school's administrator controls which data an app like this can reach, and can revoke it. If you believe a child has connected an account without appropriate consent, write to privacy@contextoagent.ai and we will delete the account and its data.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data we will update this page and move the effective date. Where a change is significant — a new category of data, or a new recipient — we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
